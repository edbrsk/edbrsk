## Get help

Email [edbrsk@gmail.com](mailto:edbrsk@gmail.com) with your questions, problems or ideas about Dreambudget.

## Delete your account and data

To delete your Dreambudget account and everything in it:

1. Email [edbrsk@gmail.com](mailto:edbrsk@gmail.com) from the address you signed up with, with the subject
   "Delete my Dreambudget account".
2. You'll get a reply confirming the request.
3. Within 30 days, your account and all the data you entered are deleted from the server: your name, email and
   password, accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, payees, entities and piggy banks. Nothing is kept
   afterwards.

To delete only part of your data, say which in the email. The data the app keeps on your phone is removed when
you log out or uninstall the app.

The [privacy policy](/dreambudget/privacy/) explains what Dreambudget collects and how it is used.
