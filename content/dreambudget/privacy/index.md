_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

## In short

- Dreambudget stores your account and the budgeting data you enter, so the app can show it to you on your devices.
- It is stored on a server in Spain run by the developer. It is not sold, shared for advertising or used to profile you.
- The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, at any time.

## Who is responsible

Dreambudget is developed and run by Edgar Ordóñez, an individual developer, who is responsible for your data
under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any question or request about your data, email
[edbrsk@gmail.com](mailto:edbrsk@gmail.com).

## What Dreambudget collects

**Your account.** Your name, email address and password, which you give when you sign up, log in or change your
password. They are used to identify you and keep your data private to your account.

**What you enter in the app.** Your accounts and their balances, transactions (amount, date, payee, category,
account, memo and flags), budgets and their targets, categories, payees, entities and piggy banks. For a payee you
can also add an email address and a postal address, which are optional.

**Stored only on your phone.** The app keeps a login token in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or
Android Keystore), and your name, email, language and theme in the app's settings, so you stay logged in and the
app looks the way you chose. They are deleted when you log out or remove the app.

Dreambudget does not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and it does not read
other apps' data.

## How it is used

Your data is used only to run Dreambudget for you: to save your budget, show it on your devices and calculate your
reports. The legal basis is the contract between you and Dreambudget when you create an account (GDPR article
6(1)(b)).

## Where it is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored on a server in Spain operated by the developer, and travels between the app and the server
over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Only the developer can access the server, and only to run, maintain and fix
the service.

Payee logos are loaded from a third-party logo service. When the app shows a payee's logo, your phone requests the
image from that service, which sees your phone's IP address and which logo was requested. None of your account or
budget data is sent to it.

If you install Dreambudget from Google Play or the App Store, those stores process data about the download under
their own privacy policies.

## How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. If you ask for your account to be deleted, your account and
everything you entered are deleted from the server within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask to see a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, or object to or
restrict how it is used. Email [edbrsk@gmail.com](mailto:edbrsk@gmail.com) from the address you signed up with,
and you'll get a reply within one month.

If you think your data has been handled wrongly, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Spain that is
the [Agencia Española de Protección de Datos](https://www.aepd.es).

## Children

Dreambudget is not meant for children under 14, and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a change affects what
is collected or who it is shared with, it will be announced in the app before it applies.
